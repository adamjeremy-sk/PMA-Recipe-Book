Contact & Support

We value your feedback and are committed to the continuous improvement of PMA Recipe Book. Whether you have encountered a bug, have a feature request, or need assistance with the application, please do not hesitate to reach out.

Our team adheres to strict architectural discipline and responsiveness. We aim to address all inquiries in a timely manner.

General Support

For all general support inquiries, account issues, or questions regarding the application's functionality, please contact us via email.

  • Email: support@weatherapplibre.ca
  • Subject Line: Please include "PMA RecipeBook Support" in your subject line for faster routing.
  • Details: Include your device model and Android version to help us diagnose issues more effectively.

Bug Reports

We rely on our community to help us identify and resolve defects. If you encounter unexpected behavior, please report it.

  • Steps to Reproduce: Clearly outline the actions that led to the issue.
  • Expected Behavior: Describe what you expected to happen.
  • Actual Behavior: Describe what actually happened.
  • Screenshots: Attach screenshots if the issue is visual or related to the UI layout.

Feature Requests

We are always looking to expand the capabilities of the Flavor Engine and the Culinary Physics database. We welcome your suggestions.

  • New Ingredients: Requests for new entries in the density or flavor vector database.
  • UI Enhancements: Suggestions for improving the "Tin" interface or theme engine.
  • Workflow Improvements: Ideas for streamlining recipe entry or scaling.

Privacy & Data

PMA Recipe Book is designed with privacy in mind. All recipe data and flavor profiles are stored locally on your device.

  • No Cloud Sync: We do not currently offer cloud synchronization, ensuring your data remains yours.
  • No Telemetry: The application does not transmit usage statistics or personal data to external servers.
  • Local Storage: Your data is secure within the application's sandboxed storage.
