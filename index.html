Feature 1: The Flavor Engine

Explore ingredients through a deterministic flavor network. Our substitution engine uses cosine similarity to find the perfect match for missing ingredients.

  • Vector Mapping: Multi-dimensional taste and aroma profiling.
  • Smart Substitutions: Mathematically backed alternatives.
  • Clickable Profiles: Instant access to ingredient data.

Feature 2: Culinary Physics

Seamlessly switch between volumetric and mass measurements. Our database handles the density calculations so you can cook with precision.

  • Unit Conversion: Cups to Grams and back again.
  • Density Lookup: Specific ingredient data for accuracy.
  • Recipe Scaling: Intelligent fraction formatting.

Feature 3: The "Tin" Interface

A bespoke Rolodex-style scrolling interface designed for hands-free navigation. Use your hardware volume buttons to flip through recipes while your hands are covered in dough.

  • Volume Navigation: Scroll without touching the screen.
  • Frictionless UI: Designed for the messy kitchen environment.
  • Portable Theme Engine: Luminance-driven palettes for any lighting.

Feature 4: Smart Recommendations

The app learns from your cooking habits. Track usage tallies and receive heuristic recommendations based on ingredients you use most frequently.

  • Usage Tracking: Mark recipes as favorites.
  • Heuristic Engine: Suggestions based on flavor affinities.
  • Personalized Feed: Recipes you'll actually cook.
