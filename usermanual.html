User Manual

Welcome to the official user manual for PMA Recipe Book. This guide covers everything from navigating the unique "Tin" interface to utilizing the Flavor Engine for perfect substitutions.

PMA Recipe Book is designed with strict architectural discipline, ensuring your data is stable, localized (English/French), and secure. Below you will find instructions for all major features.

1. Navigation & The "Tin" Interface

PMA Recipe Book abandons standard scrolling for a bespoke "Rolodex" or "Tin" experience.

  • Volume Keys: Press the volume up or down buttons to scroll through recipes. This allows for hands-free navigation when your hands are messy.
  • Touch Scroll: You can also swipe up or down on the screen to browse recipes.
  • Hamburger Menu: Tap the icon in the top-left corner to open the navigation drawer. Here you can access Settings, Search, and Preferences.
  • Visual Focus: The active recipe is always centered in the "Tin" view for clarity.

2. Managing Recipes

Create and edit recipes with precision. The Root Activity acts as a thin orchestrator; all data logic is handled securely in the background.

  • Add Recipe: Use the "+" button to create a new recipe. Enter the title, instructions, and tags (e.g., "Wok", "Fermented").
  • Ingredients: Add ingredients by name. The system automatically detects if an ingredient exists in our Flavor Vector database.
  • Search: Search by ingredient name or technique tag to find recipes quickly.
  • Tags: Use tags to organize recipes by cooking method, cuisine, or difficulty.

3. The Flavor Engine

This is the heart of PMA Recipe Book. We use a deterministic vector network to map flavors, aromas, and textures.

  • Clickable Ingredients: Tap any ingredient in a recipe to reveal its Flavor Profile. You will see its breakdown across the 5 basic tastes (Sweet, Salty, Sour, Bitter, Umami) and aroma categories (Floral, Spicy, Earthy, etc.).
  • Substitutions: If you are missing an ingredient, tap the "Substitute" button. The engine calculates the cosine similarity between vectors to suggest the best mathematical match (e.g., substituting Tarragon for Basil).
  • Flavor Network: Explore the connections between ingredients. The app visualizes which ingredients pair well based on their shared aroma and flavor profiles.

4. Culinary Physics & Scaling

Cooking is a science. Our database handles the physics so you can focus on the food.

  • Unit Conversion: Tap the unit label (e.g., "Cups") to convert to Mass (e.g., "Grams"). The app uses a localized density database to perform accurate volumetric-to-mass conversions.
  • Scaling: Use the slider to scale the recipe up or down. The app recalculates all quantities instantly.
  • Fraction Formatting: We display fractions intelligently. You will see "1/2 tsp" instead of "0.5 tsp" for better readability.
  • Density Database: We maintain a comprehensive table of ingredient densities to ensure 1 Cup of Flour always equals the correct mass, distinct from 1 Cup of Sugar.

Customize the look and feel of your application with our portable Theme Engine.

  • Themes: Open Settings to switch between themes like Crema, Charcoal, Cobalt, and Jade. The engine is luminance-driven and portable.
