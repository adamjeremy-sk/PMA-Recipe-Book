About PMA Recipe Book

PMA Recipe Book is not just a digital notepad; it is a relational culinary knowledge base and an interactive execution environment. Developed by Puppet Monkey Apps, our goal is to bring scientific precision and flavor exploration to the home kitchen.

We believe that cooking is a science as much as it is an art. By mapping ingredients to multi-dimensional flavor vectors, we allow users to explore the hidden connections between tastes, aromas, and textures.

The Flavor Engine

At the core of PMA Recipe Book is our deterministic Flavor Engine. Unlike other apps that rely on static text lists, we use a relational database of flavor vectors.

  • Vector Mapping: Every ingredient is mapped across 5 basic tastes, aroma categories, and mouthfeel textures.
  • Cosine Similarity: Substitutions are calculated mathematically. If you run out of Basil, the engine finds the ingredient with the closest vector match (e.g., Tarragon) based on your specific flavor profile.
  • No AI Hallucinations: Our system is deterministic and reliable, ensuring consistent results every time.

Culinary Physics

We treat cooking as a physics problem. Our database includes density information for thousands of ingredients, allowing for seamless conversion between volumetric and mass measurements.

  • Density Lookup: Switch between "1 Cup of Flour" and "120g of Flour" instantly.
  • Smart Scaling: Scale recipes up or down with intelligent fraction formatting (e.g., 1/2 tsp instead of 0.5 tsp).
  • Standardized Units: Support for Metric, Imperial, and US Customary units.

The "Tin" Interface

We designed the user interface to be frictionless. The "Tin" interface mimics a physical rolodex or recipe box, allowing you to scroll through recipes without touching the screen.

  • Volume Navigation: Use hardware volume keys to flip pages. Perfect for messy hands.
  • Portable Theme Engine: A luminance-driven theme system that adapts to your environment (Crema, Charcoal, Cobalt, Jade).
  • Sharp Aesthetics: A modular, cellular design with high contrast and clear separation.
