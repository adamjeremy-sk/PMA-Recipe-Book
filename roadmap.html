Development Roadmap

Welcome to the official development roadmap. Our goal is to build more than just a digital notepad; we are crafting a relational culinary knowledge base and an interactive execution environment.

Current Focus

Phase 1

We are currently architecting the Relational Culinary Database and the Volumetric-to-Mass conversion engine.

Project Status

Active

Core UI and Domain Models are established. Flavor vector mapping in progress.

Phase 1: The Foundation & The Tin

The first phase focuses on the core architectural foundation and our unique presentation layer.

  • The "Tin" Interface: Implementing the bespoke Rolodex-style scrolling interface, allowing for frictionless, hands-free navigation using hardware volume buttons.
  • Theme Engine: Integrating our portable, luminance-driven Boutique Theme Engine with multiple palettes (Crema, Charcoal, Cobalt, Jade, etc.).
  • Core Architecture: Establishing the strict domain-driven data models and localization infrastructure (English/French).

Phase 2: The Culinary Database

Building the scientific backbone of the application.

  • Relational Entities: Normalizing Recipes, Ingredients, Instructions, and Techniques into a robust local SQL database.
  • Culinary Physics: Implementing the Volumetric-to-Mass conversion database, allowing users to seamlessly switch between cups and grams based on specific ingredient densities.
  • Recipe Scaling: Mathematical scaling of recipes with intelligent fraction formatting (e.g., 1/2 tsp instead of 0.5 tsp).

Phase 3: The Flavor Engine

Where the magic happens. Moving beyond simple lists into a deterministic flavor network.

  • Flavor Vectors: Mapping every ingredient to a multi-dimensional vector covering the 5 basic tastes, aroma categories (fruity, floral, herbal, spicy, roasted, earthy, pungent), and mouthfeel.
  • Substitution Engine: Using cosine similarity to mathematically calculate and suggest the best possible substitutes for missing ingredients.
  • Clickable Ingredients: Tapping an ingredient reveals its full aromatic and flavor profile.

Phase 4: Smart Recommendations & Polish

Personalizing the experience and finalizing the application for release.

  • Usage Tallies: Tracking recipe popularity and execution logs to understand user preferences.
  • Heuristic Recommendations: Generating recipe suggestions based on frequently used ingredients and flavor affinities.
  • Monetization: Implementing the free ad-supported tier and the one-time purchase to remove ads forever.
  • Accessibility Audit: Ensuring the custom Rolodex UI and hardware navigation are fully compliant with screen readers.

Future Horizons (Backburner)

Features planned for post-launch updates.

  • Import Engine: The ability to import recipes from other popular recipe applications and websites.
  • Community Sharing: Exporting and sharing your custom flavor profiles and recipe creations.
